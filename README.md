# Qiskit Fall Fest @ UIUC

Official website and open-source resources for Qiskit Fall Fest @ UIUC.

## From Qubit to Quantum Hardware

A week-long, beginner-friendly quantum computing event at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, organized by SIGQuantum (ACM@UIUC's Special Interest Group for Quantum Information) and IBM Quantum.

Across the week we move through quantum hardware, hands-on Qiskit coding, quantum optics, advanced Qiskit applications, and the state of quantum in industry — closing with a weekend mini-hackathon. No prior quantum experience is required.

## Points & Certificates

Earn points for each event you attend by filling out the attendance forms.

- **4 points** — official IBM certificate for participating
- **10 points** — winner IBM certificate

## Mini-Hackathon

Project teams build a solid deliverable based on their chosen topic. Judging takes place during the following week's meeting, and the top 2 teams receive a winner certificate plus priority sponsored travel funding for IQuHack if desired.

- Submission before **Sunday 11:59 PM** → **3 points**
- Submission before the next meeting → **1 point**

There's no need to rush to finish — it's a fun opportunity to make progress and show off your work.
