01 / WHY THIS EVENT

Quantum computing should not feel impossible to enter.

Quantum computing is one of the most exciting emerging technologies of our time, but for many students, knowing where to begin can be difficult.

This event is designed for students who are curious about quantum computing but do not yet know how to start.

Instead of teaching quantum computing as a sequence of disconnected mathematical concepts, we will follow a quantum program through the entire system — from Python code to the physical quantum hardware that executes it.
