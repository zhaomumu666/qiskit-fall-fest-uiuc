02 / WHY THIS EVENT

We focus on quantum computing.

Quantum computing is one of the most important emerging computing paradigms, with applications across chemistry, materials, optimization and cryptography.

This event lowers the entry barrier for UIUC students: we avoid heavy math-first teaching, and take you through practical programming, circuits, algorithms and real hardware using Qiskit.

We run a full week of in-person sessions plus an optional remote final project.
